Browse benchmark collections from [EvalHub](https://github.com/eval-hub/eval-hub/tree/main/config/collections), synced at site build time. Curated collections appear first, ordered by their curation priority, followed by non-curated collections.

Use a collection ID to [run its benchmarks together](/guides/collections/). Open its definition for the full description, parameters, and scoring criteria.
